# 📊 Advanced Sales Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project delivers an end-to-end **sales analytics solution** using Power BI, combining data analysis, visualization, and business storytelling to uncover revenue drivers, profitability trends, and customer insights.

The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders **monitor performance, identify growth opportunities, and optimize business strategy**.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Businesses often face challenges in understanding:

* Which products and regions drive revenue
* Where profitability is declining
* How customer behavior impacts sales

👉 This project answers:

* What drives revenue and profit growth?
* Which products and channels are most efficient?
* Where should the business focus to maximize returns?

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

* Power BI (Dashboard & DAX)
* Excel (Data Source & Preprocessing)

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 🔹 Executive Overview & Trends

* Total Revenue: **$0.1B+**
* Total Profit: **$39.6M**
* Profit Margin: **37.5%**
* Monthly revenue and profit trends
* Order distribution and customer spending patterns

---

### 🔹 Product & Channel Performance

* Top-selling products driving revenue
* High-margin products for strategic focus
* Revenue vs Profit scatter analysis
* Channel contribution (Wholesale, Distributor, Export)
* Channel efficiency and profit pipeline

---

### 🔹 Geographic & Customer Insights

* State-wise revenue and profit distribution
* Top customers by revenue and margin
* Regional performance comparison
* Interactive map visualization

---

## 📈 Key Insights

* California contributes ~20% of total revenue, making it a key growth region
* West region shows the highest profit margin (~37.5%)
* A small group of products drives the majority of revenue
* High discounts negatively impact profitability in certain categories
* Wholesale channel generates the highest revenue but requires margin optimization

---

## 💡 Business Impact

✔ Identify high-performing regions and customers
✔ Optimize product portfolio for better profitability
✔ Improve channel efficiency and pricing strategy
✔ Enable data-driven decision-making
